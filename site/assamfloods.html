Assam Floods, 2026

Assam is facing severe flooding after unprecedented rainfall over the hill catchments of neighbouring Nagaland surged downstream into Assam's rivers. More than 7 lakh people across 11 districts have been affected. The official channel below is the Chief Minister's Relief Fund, Assam — the state government's own fund for flood relief.

We are human. So are the families wading through this right now — watching a home, a harvest, a year of work disappear in a matter of days. We don't know them, and they don't know us, but the fear of losing everything and the relief of a stranger's help don't need a shared language or a shared border to be real. This is that moment to stand by them, the way you'd want someone to stand by you.
