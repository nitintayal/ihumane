iHumane

I am human. So is the family who just lost everything they own to a crisis they never chose.

A home lost overnight. A child separated from a parent in the rush to safety. A harvest, a livelihood, a year of work — gone in an instant. Borders, languages, and distance make it easy to feel that pain for a moment and move on. But strip all of that away, and it's the same fear, the same loss, the same need to be helped by someone who didn't have to help at all. I am human. So are they. We are all one — and standing by each other in hard times isn't charity, it's just what family does.

iHumane doesn't run relief efforts itself. When a crisis hits, we find the official fund behind it, confirm it's real, and walk you straight to it — so the people who need you most actually receive what you send.
