iHumane

I am human. So is the family standing on a rooftop right now, watching everything they own disappear underwater.

A home lost overnight. A child separated from a parent in the rush to safety. A harvest, a livelihood, a year of work — gone in a single flood. Borders, languages, and distance make it easy to feel that pain for a moment and move on. But strip all of that away, and it's the same fear, the same loss, the same need to be helped by someone who didn't have to help at all. I am human. So are they. We are all one — and standing by each other in hard times isn't charity, it's just what family does.
